# AI Learning Map

This page collects public courses and learning resources related to artificial intelligence.

The goal is not to list every AI course available online, but to keep a curated map of courses that are worth studying seriously. The collection will cover foundations, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, language models, AI systems, and applied AI projects.

Some courses here are ones I have studied or am currently studying; others are courses I plan to explore later. I will keep refining this page as my understanding of the field changes.
